A short introduction.

The goal was to make research insights easier to discover, helping designers, product managers, and other stakeholders make better-informed product decisions.

### The Problem

The design team was conducting valuable research, but there was no simple way to share and manage those insights across the organisation.

Research lived across different locations, making it difficult for teams to know:

- What research already existed
- What insights were relevant to their work
- Where to find previous findings

A key challenge was creating something that people would actually use. Rather than introducing another standalone tool requiring a new login or workflow, the solution needed to fit into existing processes.

---

### Approach

The project was guided by a few key principles:

#### Reduce friction

The tool should work with existing workflows rather than creating additional overhead.

#### Empower non-technical users

The research team needed to manage content without depending on developers.

#### Improve discoverability

Finding relevant research should be quick and intuitive.

---

### From Concept to Prototype

I started by designing and presenting the concept internally to design management to gather feedback and validate the direction.

After initial feedback, I created a proof of concept to explore:

- How research could be structured
- How users would discover content
- How existing tools could support the experience

The final stage involved working with engineering and DevOps to host the solution internally and test how it could work beyond the initial group.

### Technical Approach

The main challenge was creating a custom experience while keeping content management simple.

Instead of building a separate content management system, I used Notion as the backend. This allowed the research team to continue working in a tool they already knew while the dashboard handled presentation and discovery.

The solution used:

- Notion API as the data source
- Next.js for the frontend
- Vercel for hosting
- Internal APIs for additional functionality
- Caching to improve performance

### Key Features

#### Research discovery

Users could browse and search research content through a dedicated interface.

#### Filtering

Research could be filtered by teams and categories to make relevant insights easier to find.

#### AI summaries

Longer research documents were enhanced with AI-generated summaries to help users quickly understand key findings.

#### Notion integration

The dashboard automatically pulled content from Notion, allowing researchers to manage content without needing technical knowledge.

---

### Challenges & Learnings

Some areas that required exploration:

- Creating a useful structure for research content
- Balancing flexibility with consistency
- Working with Notion as a backend
- Designing a system that could be maintained without developer involvement
- Exploring where AI could support research discovery

---

### Reflection

This project explored how design, engineering, and existing tools can come together to solve internal workflow problems.

The biggest learning was that improving access to information is often less about creating new systems and more about understanding the tools and processes teams already use.
