### Overview

Summaries is a personal side project exploring how AI can make discovering books faster and more accessible.

The idea came from my own reading habits. When browsing bookstores, I often find interesting books but don't always know if they are worth adding to my reading list. I wanted a simple way to quickly understand what a book was about, save interesting titles, and decide what to explore further.

### Why I Built It

After completing a React course, I wanted to apply what I had learned by building something practical rather than another tutorial project.

I had previously used services like Blinkist and other short-form reading platforms, but I wanted a simpler version focused on my own workflow.

The goal was not to replace these services, but to create a lightweight tool I could use myself while exploring modern frontend development and AI integration.

---

### How It Works

The experience is designed around a simple flow:

1. Find a book
2. Generate a summary
3. Decide whether to save it or skip it
4. Keep a personal collection of interesting reads

### Exploring AI Integration

One of the interesting challenges was making AI output feel consistent.

Instead of simply displaying generated text, I explored how to guide the model towards returning structured content that could reliably be displayed in the interface.

This involved experimenting with:

- Prompt structure
- Formatting requirements
- Handling inconsistent responses
- Creating a predictable user experience

### Reflection

Summaries started as a way to practise React, but became an opportunity to explore how AI can support small personal workflows.

The biggest learning was that adding AI to an application is less about generating content and more about creating a reliable experience around unpredictable outputs.
