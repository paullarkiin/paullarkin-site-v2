For the first time in years, I finally have a new website.

I’ve been working on it for the past few months, bringing together ideas and elements from previous versions while exploring a new visual direction and approach.

I wanted to create something that reflects the journey I’ve been on, the work I enjoy doing, and where I’m heading next—something I’m genuinely proud to share.

It feels great to have a proper place on the internet again: somewhere to collect my work, experiments, thoughts, and everything in between.

The site will continue to evolve, but for now, I hope you have as much fun exploring it as I had making it.
